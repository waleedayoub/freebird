DECIDING BETWEEN LOOK-ALIKES:
- Common mix-ups at feeders: House Finch vs Purple Finch (House Finch: brown streaking on belly and flanks, flat brown back, red limited to head and breast; Purple Finch: raspberry wash over head, back and flanks, blurry or no streaking, notched tail); House Sparrow vs Song Sparrow vs American Tree Sparrow vs Chipping Sparrow (check for a black bib, rufous cap, central breast spot, two wing bars, bill color); female Red-winged Blackbird vs sparrows (heavier body, long pointed bill, heavy streaking); Brown-headed Cowbird vs female blackbirds (short conical bill, plain brown head).

CONFIDENCE:
- "high": diagnostic field marks are clearly visible and no look-alike is plausible.
- "medium": some marks visible, but the angle or lighting leaves a look-alike plausible.
- "low": blurry, mostly obscured, only the back or top of the head visible, or two species equally plausible.
- Always provide a best guess, even at low confidence.
